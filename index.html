What we suggest

Do
Run the irrigation pump between 11 am and 1 pmSolar surplus is highest then. Moving the pump saves about 14 kWh of battery cycling.
Prepare
Charge the battery to 95% before 2 pmCloud cover will cut afternoon generation, and demand rises after sunset.
Avoid
Do not schedule the flour mill after 6 pmIt would overlap the evening peak and force a 45-minute diesel start.

Common questions

Why does the power drop in the evening?

After sunset the village runs on the battery, and everyone uses lights at the same time. Run big machines between 11 am and 1 pm instead.

What should I do in a power cut?

Switch off heavy appliances so they do not start suddenly when power returns, then save a report below.

Is my personal information safe?

Your account stays on this device and your password is never saved as plain text. Village pages never show any home’s private details.
