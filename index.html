What we suggest

Do
Run the irrigation pump between 11 am and 1 pmSolar surplus is highest then. Moving the pump saves about 14 kWh of battery cycling.
Prepare
Charge the battery to 95% before 2 pmCloud cover will cut afternoon generation, and demand rises after sunset.
Avoid
Do not schedule the flour mill after 6 pmIt would overlap the evening peak and force a 45-minute diesel start.

Common questions

What is Guest Access?

You can look at village information without an account. Personal household details need a login.

Why does it say “sample data”?

This website is a demo. It is not connected to real meters yet, so the numbers are examples.

Is my personal information safe?

Your account is saved only on this device, and your PIN is never saved as plain text. Village pages never show any household’s private details.

What should I do in a power cut?

Switch off heavy appliances so they do not start suddenly when power returns, then report the problem below.

Why does the power sometimes drop in the evening?

After sunset the village runs on the battery, and everyone uses lights and appliances at the same time. Run heavy appliances between 11 am and 1 pm instead.

When is the best time to run the pump or mill?

Late morning to early afternoon, when solar output is highest. The AI prediction page shows the best hours for each day.

What does the battery percentage mean?

It shows how much stored energy is left. Above 55% power is good, below 35% please save electricity.

How is my bill calculated?

Your bill is the units (kWh) you used on your meter multiplied by the tariff. You can see both on the Account page.

How do I change my PIN?

Open Account, go to Change PIN, and enter your current and new PIN. If you forgot it, call the helpline.
